# Customer Escalation Policy

Document ID: ATL-POL-CX-003
Department: Customer Experience
Document type: Policy
Version: 2.3
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Director of Customer Success

## Purpose

This policy defines when and how Atlas Logistics communicates operational incidents to customers and internal customer owners.

## Priority customer incidents

Every priority customer incident that may affect delivery must be communicated to the assigned Customer Success Manager.
The dispatcher must notify the Customer Success Manager within 30 minutes after confirming the risk.
If the shipment is expected to miss its SLA, the Customer Success Manager must be notified before the SLA expires.

## Standard customer incidents

For a standard customer shipment, the Customer Success Manager must be notified when an SLA breach is expected to exceed 2 hours.
The notification must be sent early enough for the Customer Success Manager to prepare the customer communication.

## Customer communication

The Customer Success Manager owns external communication unless the customer account plan assigns another named owner.
The first customer update must state the shipment status, known cause, revised arrival estimate, corrective action, and time of the next update.
Unverified causes must be described as under investigation.
The customer must not be promised compensation before the required financial approval has been obtained.

## Update frequency

Priority customers with an active SLA-critical incident must receive an update at least every hour.
Other customers with an active SLA-critical incident must receive an update at least every 2 hours.
A material change to the arrival estimate or corrective action must be communicated without waiting for the next scheduled update.

## Records

All customer notifications must be recorded in the customer relationship management system.
The record must include the timestamp, recipient, sender, communication channel, message summary, and promised next update.

## Related documents

Operational escalation thresholds are defined in the Shipment SLA Policy.
Approval limits for penalties, credits, and other compensation are defined in the Financial Approval Policy.
