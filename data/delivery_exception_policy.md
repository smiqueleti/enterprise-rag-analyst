# Delivery Exception Policy

Document ID: ATL-POL-OPS-007
Department: Transport Operations
Document type: Policy
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2026-01-15
Owner: Regional Operations Director

## Purpose

This policy defines the response to events that threaten or prevent delivery within the applicable SLA.

## Exception categories

Delivery exceptions include vehicle breakdown, severe weather, road closure, border delay, customs hold, driver unavailability, damaged cargo, refused delivery, and incorrect delivery information.
The dispatcher must use the most specific available exception category in the transport management system.

## Immediate response

The dispatcher must contact the carrier within 15 minutes after confirming an SLA-critical exception.
The carrier must provide a revised arrival estimate and corrective action within 30 minutes of contact.
The dispatcher must assess alternative vehicles, routes, cross-docking, and expedited delivery when operationally feasible.

## Incident ownership

The dispatcher owns the exception until it is resolved or formally transferred.
The Regional Operations Manager becomes the incident owner after an escalation under the Shipment SLA Policy.
The incident owner must assign actions and set the next review time.

## Update frequency

SLA-critical standard shipments must be reviewed at least every hour.
SLA-critical priority customer shipments must be reviewed at least every 30 minutes.
Each review must record the latest arrival estimate, corrective-action status, and any change in financial exposure.

## Closure

An exception may be closed after proof of delivery is recorded or the shipment is formally cancelled.
The incident owner must record the final cause, actual delay, actions taken, customer impact, and any financial consequence.
Exceptions that cause an SLA breach of more than 4 hours require a root-cause review within 2 business days.

## Related documents

Customer communications must follow the Customer Escalation Policy.
Financial commitments and penalty approvals must follow the Financial Approval Policy.
