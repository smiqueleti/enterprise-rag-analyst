# Shipment SLA Policy

Document ID: ATL-POL-LOG-001
Department: Logistics
Document type: Policy
Version: 2.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Owner: Director of European Logistics

## Purpose

This policy defines delivery service-level agreements and escalation thresholds for Atlas Logistics road shipments across Europe.

## Scope

This policy applies from dispatch confirmation until proof of delivery is recorded.
It applies to standard and priority customer shipments handled by Atlas Logistics or an approved carrier.

## Service-level agreements

The standard delivery SLA is 24 hours from dispatch confirmation.
The priority customer delivery SLA is 18 hours from dispatch confirmation.
The applicable SLA must be recorded in the transport management system before dispatch confirmation.

## Delay classification

A shipment is considered at risk when its predicted arrival is more than 2 hours later than its planned arrival.
A shipment is considered SLA-critical when the latest predicted arrival is later than the applicable SLA deadline.
Dispatchers must use the latest carrier update and available tracking data when calculating the predicted delay.

## Escalation thresholds

A standard shipment must be escalated to the Regional Operations Manager when its predicted delay exceeds 4 hours.
A priority customer shipment must be escalated to the Regional Operations Manager when its predicted delay exceeds 2 hours.
Escalation is required even when a corrective action is expected to prevent an SLA breach.

## Required records

The dispatcher must record the delay reason, predicted delay, proposed corrective action, responsible owner, and next review time in the transport management system.
The dispatcher must update the record whenever the predicted arrival changes by 30 minutes or more.
