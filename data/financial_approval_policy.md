# Financial Approval Policy

Document ID: ATL-POL-FIN-009
Department: Finance
Document type: Policy
Version: 1.8
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Owner: European Finance Director

## Purpose

This policy defines approval limits for customer compensation, contractual penalties, expedited transport, and other unplanned shipment costs.

## General rules

Financial exposure must be estimated as soon as a delivery exception indicates that an additional cost or customer claim is reasonably possible.
Splitting one financial commitment into multiple smaller commitments to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited.
Approval must be recorded before Atlas Logistics communicates or accepts a binding financial commitment.

## Approval thresholds

Dispatch Supervisors may approve unplanned operational costs up to and including EUR 500.
Regional Operations Managers may approve unplanned operational costs above EUR 500 and up to and including EUR 5,000.
The European Finance Director must approve unplanned operational costs above EUR 5,000.

Potential contractual penalties up to and including EUR 1,000 must be recorded but do not require advance approval unless compensation is offered to the customer.
Potential contractual penalties above EUR 1,000 require approval from the Regional Operations Manager.
Potential contractual penalties above EUR 10,000 also require approval from the European Finance Director.

## Expedited transport

An expedited transport option may be approved when it is expected to reduce the total combination of operational cost, contractual penalty, and customer impact.
The incident owner must document the available options, estimated costs, expected delay reduction, and selected option.
Urgency does not remove the approval requirement.

## Customer compensation

The Customer Success Manager may propose a credit or other compensation but may not make a binding offer without approval under this policy.
Approved compensation must be recorded in both the customer relationship management system and the incident record.

## Related documents

Customer communication is governed by the Customer Escalation Policy.
