# Dispatch Operations SOP

Document ID: ATL-SOP-OPS-004
Department: Transport Operations
Document type: SOP
Version: 1.4
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Owner: Head of Dispatch Operations

## Purpose

This procedure defines the checks and records required before and after a road shipment is dispatched.

## Pre-dispatch checks

The dispatcher must confirm the delivery address, requested delivery window, shipment priority, assigned carrier, vehicle identifier, and driver contact details.
The dispatcher must verify that the carrier has accepted the route before confirming dispatch.
The dispatcher must confirm that all required customs and dangerous-goods documents are available when applicable.

## Dispatch confirmation

Dispatch confirmation may be recorded only after the shipment has left the origin facility.
The dispatch confirmation timestamp starts the delivery SLA clock.
The transport management system must contain the applicable SLA and planned arrival time at confirmation.

## Monitoring

Active shipments must be reviewed at least every 2 hours.
Priority customer shipments must be reviewed at least every hour.
The dispatcher must investigate when tracking data has not been received for 60 minutes.

## Delay handling

When a shipment becomes at risk, the dispatcher must validate the predicted arrival with the carrier.
The dispatcher must record the cause of the delay and request a corrective action from the carrier.
The dispatcher must follow the escalation thresholds in the Shipment SLA Policy.
The dispatcher must follow the incident response steps in the Delivery Exception Policy if the shipment is expected to miss its SLA.

## Handover

An unresolved at-risk shipment must be included in the written shift handover.
The handover must identify the shipment, current status, predicted delay, actions taken, next action, owner, and next review time.
The outgoing dispatcher remains responsible until the incoming dispatcher acknowledges the handover.
